val scale_channels : width_mult:Base.Float.t -> int -> Base__Int.t

scale_channels ~width_mult channels is a channel width scaled by a MobileNet-style width multiplier: channels *. width_mult rounded to the NEAREST multiple of 8, a tie (an exact odd multiple of 4) rounding up, and never below 8. So scale_channels ~width_mult:0.75 32 = 24, scale_channels ~width_mult:0.6 64 = 40 (38.4 rounds up), scale_channels ~width_mult:0.6 32 = 16 (19.2 rounds down), scale_channels ~width_mult:1.125 32 = 40 (the tie 36 rounds up), and scale_channels ~width_mult:0.1 32 = 8 (the floor). Multiples of 8 keep channel axes aligned with SIMD lanes and tensor-core tiles. Unlike the MobileNet reference _make_divisible, there is no extra round-up when rounding down loses more than 10%: the nearest multiple is the whole rule. Raises Invalid_argument unless width_mult is finite and positive.

val mobile_cnn : label:Base.string Base.list -> ?num_classes:??? -> ?width_mult:??? -> unit -> train_step:'a option -> Ocannl_tensor.Tensor.t -> Ocannl_tensor.Tensor.t

Modern CNN with depthwise separable convolutions for efficiency, after MobileNet v1's first blocks. Suitable for mobile/edge deployment.

The channel widths at width_mult = 1.0 are 32 (the initial stride-2 conv), then 64, 128, 128 and 256 (the four depthwise separable blocks, at strides 1, 2, 1, 2). Every one of these internal widths is scaled by width_mult through scale_channels (nearest multiple of 8, ties up, at least 8), and the final batch norm and the classifier's input follow the last scaled width. The external dimensions are not scaled: the input channels are whatever the input has, and the output has num_classes logits. Raises Invalid_argument unless width_mult is finite and positive.
