Module Bench_select

Two-stage selection of a class's best arm from noisy timings (gh-ocannl-1531).

A harness that times many candidates, one round per geometry, and then wants "the best candidate of a class" faces the winner's curse: the arm that reads best in its own round is, more often than its true rank warrants, the one whose batches drew the least contention, and the round it was read in is not the round its rivals were read in. projection_shape_bench used to send exactly that arm -- the round-best under each statistic -- to its finalists round, so a true best that read second in its geometry round never reached the round that decides (staging#1331: a b8x128 coal-k finalist slower than the one staging#1209 had crowned).

The rule here is two stages:

  • shortlist: from the first readings, keep the top k under the PRIMARY statistic and the best under each other statistic. It contains every arm the single-shot rule would have sent, so the change can only add contenders.
  • the harness re-times the whole shortlist in ONE interleaved round, in one process, so every contender of the class is read under the same drift;
  • best: the finalists are the best under each statistic by the RE-TIMED readings alone. The first readings only decided who is re-timed; mixing them back in would reintroduce the cross-round drift the re-timing exists to remove.

The harness then times the finalists once more, so the number it reports is not the draw that selected them. Scores are "higher is better" (throughput); a NaN ranks below every number, and ties go to the earlier arm, so a selection is a function of its inputs. The property test test/operations/bench_select drives both rules with scripted noise.
