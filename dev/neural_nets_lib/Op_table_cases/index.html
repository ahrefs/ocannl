type arm =
  1. | Unary of Ops.unop
  2. | Binary of Ops.binop
  3. | Ternary of Ops.ternop
  4. | Vector of Ops.vec_unop
  5. | Convert_from of Ops.prec
  6. | Inlined of arm
    (*

    A homogeneous arithmetic arm whose first operand is an inlined a + b rather than a buffer read: C-family integer promotion types that sum int where the read is uchar, so an arm that compiles over a read can still be an ambiguous call over an expression.

    *)
  7. | Inlined_real of arm
    (*

    A homogeneous arithmetic arm at an integer storage precision whose first operand is an inlined real-valued Sqrt(a): a float-valued subexpression at an integer compute precision, which the renderer computes in float and converts once (gh-ocannl-1785), so an integer arm never meets a float operand -- no mixed min(uchar, double), no % over a double.

    *)
  8. | Under_mod of arm
    (*

    A homogeneous arithmetic arm at an integer storage precision as the first operand of Mod, the one operator whose integer spelling takes integers only: an arm that is float-TYPED over its integral value (cc's fmax, truncf and ? 1.0 : 0.0, Metal's clamp((float)...)) compiles into a store and fails here (gh-ocannl-1883).

    *)
  9. | Lanes of arm
    (*

    A homogeneous arithmetic arm applied cell by cell over a Vectorized loop, every operand read at the loop index: what the explicit-SIMD rendering takes (gh-ocannl-1768).

    *)
  10. | Lanes_uniform of arm
    (*

    Lanes with the last operand a loop-free read: the rendering's splat of a lane-uniform value, and the only form a gated operand may take in a lane.

    *)
  11. | Reduce of Ops.binop
    (*

    acc = op(acc, x[i]) over a Vectorized loop: the SIMD accumulator grid.

    *)
  12. | Reduce_fma
    (*

    acc = fma(x[i], y[i], acc) over a Vectorized loop.

    *)
