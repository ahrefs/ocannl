Module Test_utils.Tensorized_census_scan

Finding executed claims about a tensorized routine whose item judges no mma census, which parity alone cannot replace (gh-ocannl-1309).

The reader behind test/operations/tensorized_census_ratchet (gh-ocannl-1309): an executed claim about a TENSORIZED routine needs a census judgment beside it.

A schedule that asks for tensorization and declines at codegen renders the lane-0 scalar fallback, which computes the same values. So a parity claim over an executed tensorized candidate -- "the packed+tensorized matmul matches the serial twin bitwise" -- passes whether the tensor-core path ran or not; only the compiled routine's mma census (Context.routine.mma, gh-ocannl-626) tells the two apart. gh-ocannl-1283 found eleven tests whose tensorized claims rested on parity alone and gave each a census claim; this reader stops the twelfth.

The rule

A Verdict claim whose label names tensorization AND asserts parity, in a top-level structure item that executes a routine, needs a census JUDGMENT in that item. "In that item" includes the file's own top-level bindings the item references, transitively, so a helper that executes (or judges) on the item's behalf counts for it. A claim in a top-level helper is ALSO executed by every item that references the helper, transitively (let check got = Verdict.p … called as check got after an execution), and each such executing item must judge within its own reach (which includes the helper's). A tensorized claim that asserts no parity -- "the tensorized seeds are proposed", "the twin constructs and validates" -- is about structure, which the census does not decide, and is not read.

The closed lists of recognised forms

  • A claim is an application, by its path, of a Verdict.Claims member other than fail (whose argument is a message) and skipped (which decides nothing), that Verdict_callee resolves as Verdict's or as ambiguous. Its label is its first positional argument -- the format, for pf/claimf/pf_all2.
  • A label's fragments are the string literals written in the label expression, and those of every name it references, followed through let bindings in scope (a function parameter and a function-valued binding contribute none). Both tests below are case-insensitive and pass when SOME fragment passes, not necessarily the same one.
  • A label names tensorization by the substrings tensoriz (tensorized, tensorizes, tensorization), tensor core or tensor-core, or the words mma or wmma, where a word is bounded by a non-letter or the string's end (a plural s allowed), so Tile_mma, sk_mma and qkv_mma: are words and comma or gemma are not.
  • A label asserts parity by a word starting match, bitwise, parity or reference, or the phrases within tolerance or same values.
  • An item executes when it references one of: Ll_test.execute, execute_with_census, run, run_linked, run_routine, optimize_and_execute; Context.run; Train.run, run_once, forward_once, update_once. The Ll_test ones are pinned to the harness: the ratchet derives the top-level bindings of test/support/ll_test.ml that reach Context.run (executors_of_harness) and fails when the two sets differ.
  • An item judges the census when it references one of the census PREDICATES -- Ll_test.rendered_tensorized, all_tensorized, all_declined, all_rendered, none_emitted, census_all, census_agrees_with_capability; C_syntax.equal_tensorization, compare_tensorization, is_tensorized_rendering, equal_mma_rendering, compare_mma_rendering -- writes one of the census's constructors, in an expression or a pattern (Tensorized, Scalar_fallback, Not_requested, Mma_intrinsics, Mma_intrinsics_ldmatrix, Mma_register_tiled, Mma_scalar_fallback), unqualified or qualified by C_syntax, or reads one of the census's fields (Ir.C_syntax.mma_summary's tensorization, scalar_fallbacks, renderings; Autotune.report's best_tensorization, best_mma_scalar_fallbacks), unqualified or qualified by C_syntax or Autotune. Obtaining the census is not judging it: execute_with_census, Ll_test.renderings and a read of routine.Context.mma count only when something above decides on the value, since a census bound and discarded (Some (got, _)) is exactly the parity-only shape.

A form is matched by its module and name. The module is the qualifier's last component (Ir.C_syntax, Test_utils.Ll_test), or what a local module L = Ll_test alias denotes; an open (structure-level or local) of one of the modules above brings that module's forms into unqualified scope. Names are resolved lexically (Lexical_scope): a parameter or local binding shadowing a form, or a top-level name, is that binding instead, and a module the source binds to a structure (or a functor parameter, an unpack) under a library's name is the source's own. A label's fragments are resolved the same way: a name the label expression binds itself shadows the outer one.

What it deliberately does not read

Each is a boundary, stated so a miss past it is a known deferral:

  • Granularity is the top-level item. A census judgment anywhere in the item (or a helper it references) covers every tensorized claim in it, even one about a different execution.
  • A claim reached through a wrapper the source defines (let mma_gated label b = gated … label b) is read at the wrapper's own claim, whose label is a parameter with no fragments -- so the wrapper's call sites are not read.
  • A label whose tensorization word arrives through a function parameter (Printf.sprintf "%s: every %s candidate matches" tag what with what a parameter) has no fragment that says so, and the claim is not a tensorized claim to this reader. The same holds for a claim whose label never says "tensorized" although its routine was a tensorized seed: the label is the closed signal this reader has.
  • An execution through anything other than the forms above -- Autotune's validation of a candidate, a backend's own run -- does not make an item executing.
  • A judgment the item receives as a function parameter (leg ~check where the caller passes a census check) is outside the item, so such an item's tensorized parity claim is refused -- the loud direction; no item in the tree has the shape today.
  • Reading is not deciding: a census field read only to print it, a source's own variant with a Tensorized constructor, or a census predicate whose result is dropped counts as a judgment. Each needs code written against the rule's intent.
