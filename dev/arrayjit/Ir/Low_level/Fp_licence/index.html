Module Low_level.Fp_licence

The licences of the rounding-changing algebra over the reals (gh-ocannl-998), one field per licence, each true when that family of rewrites may run:

  • contract: fusing a floating a * b + c into one FMA (one rounding instead of two);
  • reassociate: constant reassociation through Add/Mul, re-parenthesized subtraction chains, the multiplication/division interchanges, and unrolling known integer powers into multiplications;
  • finite_math: the zero identities x + 0 = x, 0 - x = -x and 0 * x = 0, which hold only for finite operands and ignoring the sign of zero.

Integer precisions take every licence but the floating-only arms (the interchanges and contraction): reassociation is exact in wrapping arithmetic, and the index canonicalizer relies on it.
