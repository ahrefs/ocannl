# Fused attention backward and block-tiled online attention

Issues: [#1002](https://github.com/ahrefs/ocannl/issues/1002) (fused attention backward) and
[#1003](https://github.com/ahrefs/ocannl/issues/1003) (block-tiled online attention); both extend
the online-softmax rewrite of [#483](gh-ocannl-483.md) and use the scan construct of
[#696](gh-ocannl-696.md).

**Date**: 2026-09-28
**Status**: **proposed / in progress** — PR 0 (detaching the softmax max) is landing with this
record. Adapted from a Codex proposal of the same date; the decision record on #1002 (last
comment, agreed with Łukasz) supersedes that proposal wherever they disagree, and the
[Decisions](#decisions) section below reproduces it.

## Recommendation and order

Detach the softmax max first; implement **1002** as a third shape of `Ir.Online_softmax` with a
portable, race-free backward; fix the hoisted nests' schedule and re-measure; then implement
**1003** as a single-pass block fold emitted by the same member. Keep independent gates and
acceptance criteria. A correct fused backward does not depend on tiling the forward; a tiled
forward does not depend on generating an adjoint of a scan. Targets: **1002** computes `dQ`,
`dK`, `dV` in complete training steps without allocating the query-by-key probability-gradient
and score-gradient intermediates, the score placement decision unchanged; **1003** computes
score and probability/value tiles on matrix units with bounded on-chip scratch and row state
between key blocks, both contractions shown tensorized and measured against the composed and
online forms.

Do not make the broader `Forall`/`Scatter_accum` taxonomy, arbitrary scan adjoints, inter-block
parallel prefix scans, a combine licence on `Scan_loop` (#1122) or schedule-time block geometry
(#1123) prerequisites. They are outside these issues' necessary scope.

## Findings from the current implementation

Grounded in a probe of `Train.grad_update` over the `online_softmax` test's leg-6 model (one
attention block over a masked ramp, raw and optimized lowerings with the rewrite on and off, head
width 8 — under the recompute cap — and 32, `gpt2_mini`'s), and in a reading of
`Schedule.tensorize_llc`.

Merely permitting `Split` inside it will not produce the desired fused block kernel. |
| Its scan writes the original row `max` and normalizer cells on each iteration. | These are repeated writes to row-sized storage, not a saved query-by-key trajectory. Preserve the final row values for downstream consumers. |

Two historical prerequisites are already resolved in this checkout: the recognizer edge cases in

## Decisions

From the decision record on #1002 (2026-09-28), after comparing the issue analyses with the Codex
proposal.

1. **Detach the softmax max first**, as its own small PR (PR 0): `let max_vals = NTDSL.O.(x_scaled
   @^^ spec)` in `Nn_blocks.softmax`, and the same idiom in `cross_entropy_loss`. An `NTDSL`
   operation over a differentiable operand cuts the gradient at that operation (`Prohibit_grad`)
   with no copy; `stop_gradient` is its identity instance. `docs/syntax_extensions.md` gains that
   sentence. Verified: compiles under `%op`, `runtest-online_softmax` green with its golden
   unchanged, the `max_vals.grad` and `cond` nests gone. Goldens that move are re-promoted.
2. **The fused backward is a third shape of `Ir.Online_softmax`**, anchored incrementally on the
   normalizer record and the hoisted reduction (`O`, `v`; `dO` from the dP reduction; `q`, `k`
   from `x`'s definition chain), not a whole-region attention descriptor: cheaper, reuses the
   forward's walkers, robust to model variants (RoPE, cross-attention, the workshop block), with
   the forward's decline discipline. It **declines outside the combined routine** — no
   cross-routine provenance seam; the project aims for big kernels. It requires the post-detach
   form. Emission: the `D = Σ_e dO·O` per-row reduction, then a dQ nest (rows outer) and a dK+dV
   nest (keys outer) with `p`, `dp`, `ds` as scope locals outside the channel loops, the mask
   chain applied to `ds` only (a finite fill keeps nonzero `p` and `dV`). Gate
   `online_softmax_backward` (`Code_borne`, default off, in `approximate`, inert without the
   forward key).
3. **Stage 0 re-measurement and the stage-1 schedule fix** — the hoisted nests' inner parallel
   loop mapped to `Workgroup` lanes under the serial key loop — land before the training
   measurement, since the hoist demotes the value pass from `(b,s,h,e)`- to `(b,s,h)`-parallel.
4. **#1003 ships the single-pass block fold**: per query block a serial scan over key blocks
   carrying `(m, l)` per row and the numerator `U` as an undeclared `Local` tile node accumulated
   by `Tile_mma` and rescaled by α between blocks. Sound by the scan's existing conservatism:
   scan-body writes are refused as virtualization candidates (`Non_virtual 148`), and schedule
   ops do not enter a scan body. Store/reload around the rescale in v1; a fragment-resident
   rescale is a later measurable step. Block size is a rewrite-time key `online_softmax_block`
   (0 = today's form) under the existing `online_softmax` gate — no third key. Scalar-expanded
   `U` is rejected: thousands of carries per scan, and MMA fragments cannot be named as scalars.
5. **Postponed, each needing a justification**: #1122 (combine licence on `Scan_loop` + declared
   carried-through-memory nodes) and #1123 (block geometry chosen at the scheduling seam).
6. The Codex validation and measurement tables are adopted as written; its provenance seam and
   max-gradient matching are dropped.

## Shared foundation: recognition anchored on the forward rewrite

Both new shapes live in `Ir.Online_softmax`, run in the tier's fixpoint, and reuse its
nest/signature/effect-census machinery. Preserve its use of node-axis relationships rather than
loop symbol equality or tensor labels. The current effect census already includes scope bodies
and recognizes `Tile_mma` as a code-motion barrier; retain that coverage. Do not recognize the
pattern merely because nodes have familiar names or matching dimensions.

The backward's anchors are facts the forward member already established in the same routine: the
`normalizer` record (`m`, `l`, `x`, rows, `t`) and the hoisted value-pass reduction (its target
names `O`, its other operand `v`). Everything else is reached from them. A routine without a
normalizer — a backward compiled separately from its forward — has nothing to anchor on and
declines unchanged. A session-scoped registry of forward normalizers (the species of the `minted`
memo, reset by `Rewrites.reset`) is a follow-up only if such a flow turns up in practice.

Saved-state contract:

- Keep the existing row `(m,l)` nodes and their final meanings. Reconstruct
  `p = exp(x - m) / l`; this avoids introducing a logarithm and cancellation in `x - (m + log l)`.
  A saved logsumexp may be evaluated later, under its own numerical evidence.
- `O` is materialized in the combined routine (read by `w_o`'s forward and backward), so
  `D = rowsum(dO * O)` reads it directly; no fallback reduction through `V` is needed.
- Preserve explicit materialization and additional consumers. Remove internal definitions only
  after proving the region is closed. If a requested intermediate or an extra gradient consumer
  prevents fusion, retain it or decline the region. A partially removed graph is not a fallback.

Every new shape consumes its matched pattern, mints stable scratch identities through `minted`,
and stays idempotent individually and together with the other shapes. A tiled forward keeps the
composed backward valid when the backward gate is off or recognition declines.

## 1002: remove quadratic backward intermediates

### Algebra and scope

For `P = softmax(S)` and `O = PV`, use:

```text
D_i       = sum_e dO_ie * O_ie
p_ij      = exp(S_ij - m_i) / l_i
dp_ij     = sum_e dO_ie * V_je
ds_ij     = chain(p_ij * (dp_ij - D_i))
dV_je    += p_ij * dO_ie
dQ_id    += ds_ij * K_jd
dK_jd    += ds_ij * Q_id
```

`chain` is the score expression's recovered elementwise backward — the mask `where`, the scale
and temperature — applied to `ds` only. These identities and probability recomputation are the
standard [FlashAttention backward](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.14135). In the composed
lowering's terms, `n.grad = e.grad * e = p * (dP - D)` with `D[s] = Σ_t p·dP = Σ_e dO·O`.

Apply the score expression's actual chain rule. With `where(mask, scaled_dot, fill)`, the masked
score branch contributes no `dQ`/`dK`; a finite fill can nevertheless produce nonzero probabilities
and `dV`. Keep mask placement relative to temperature unchanged. In particular, treating every
masked probability as zero would be wrong for finite fills and fully masked rows.

Start with the ordinary no-dropout attention region used by `bench_gpt`, constant scale and
temperature, and the layouts the recognizer can prove. Active dropout and arbitrary probability
postprocessing decline as a whole in the first version. Supporting dropout later requires
replaying the identical random mask and its inverse-keep scaling in both forward and adjoint;
the current forward hoist's permissive elementwise-chain test is insufficient for backward.

Keep sufficient precision in probability normalization, `D`, `dp`, and gradient accumulation:
normally at least f32 for narrow inputs, f64 for double. Check arithmetic precision as well as
the state node's storage precision. `p(dP - D)` versus the composed `(dP/l + dl)·e`
reassociates, and `D` comes from `dO·O` instead of `Σ_t p·dP`: within rounding, the forward's
numerics tier. The elementwise chain is kept, so a masked cell yields exactly the composed 0; a
fully masked row is NaN in both forms. Include ties in numerical tests rather than assuming
exact equality.

### Recognition

In the post-detach composed backward (`dO` = the attention output's gradient, `e` = `exp_vals`,
`dP` = `softmax.grad`, `dS` = the raw scores' gradient), the nests in program order are: A,
`dP[s,t] += dO[s,e] * v[t,e]`; B, `v.grad[t,e] += p[s,t] * dO[s,e]`; C1/D/C2, `e.grad` from
`dP / l` plus the broadcast of `l.grad[s] += dP·(-e / l²)`; E, `n.grad = e.grad * e`; F,
`x.grad += n.grad`; I/J, the mask `where` and the scale into `dS`; K and L, the `q.grad` and
`k.grad` contractions. The member matches:

1. the hoisted value-pass reduction, naming `O` and `v`;
2. A: the reduction over `e` into a `[rows, t]`-signed node reading `v` against a node with `O`'s
   signature, which names `dO`;
3. the chain C1/D/C2/E defining `n.grad` from `dP`, `e` and `l`;
4. an elementwise chain (mask `where`, the scale, anything pointwise) from `n.grad` to the node K
   and L consume against `q` and `k` — the score reduction's operands, known from `x`'s
   definition chain;
5. B reading `p` (normalized from `l`) against `dO`.

Decline conditions, in the forward's style: program order of the span, single writers, no other
reader of the consumed gradient nodes, no opaque statement in the span, one precision, live loop
ranges, read windows, and the max-gradient nests absent. A model with the composed max gradient
declines rather than being matched in two variants.

### Write ownership and loop structure

A query-parallel loop that updates all three gradients races on `dK` and `dV`. Use two owned
regions after the row-sized `D` preparation (a per-row reduction into a minted node of `m`'s
shape):

| Region | Parallel owner | Work inside the owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dQ` | batch/head/query row or query tile | Iterate keys; compute `p`, `dp`, `ds` once per pair and update the owned `dQ` channels. |
| `dK` + `dV` | batch/head/key row or key tile | Iterate queries; compute `p`, `dp`, `ds` once per pair and update the owned `dK` and `dV` channels together. |

```text
for (b,h,s) { for t { p; dp; ds; for d { q.grad[s,d] += ds * k[t,d] } } }
for (b,h,t) { for s { p; dp; ds; for d { k.grad[t,d] += ds * q[s,d] };
                                 for e { v.grad[t,e] += p * dO[s,e] } } }
```

This duplicates pairwise score/`dp` recomputation between the two regions (the forward's
two-pass trade again; the two computations are bitwise-equal per cell), but has deterministic
ownership, needs no atomics, and creates no query-by-key partial-gradient workspace. Keep each
pair's scalars outside the channel loops, so inlining cannot multiply a dot product's cost by
the output width. Start with ordinary nests; explicit block scratch and MMA reuse can follow
the machinery from 1003. Do not claim the row-wise version is already a fast GPU backward: both
nests have the hoisted shape noted in the findings and depend on the stage-1 schedule fix.

As implemented, the `dK` + `dV` region is two nests over the same owner, each with one channel
loop after its scalars, so that the stage-1 lane geometry can read them; `dp` stays a serial
reduction in the dQ and dK preambles, which keeps those two nests out of lanes (see
[As implemented](#as-implemented-pr-1)).

Preserve accumulation into gradient nodes shared with other graph paths, including `Q == K`
and tied parameters. Reuse the original zeroing boundaries; neither fused region may erase
another contribution. A workgroup barrier cannot synchronize the two globally dependent stages:
use separate segments where required, respecting the existing fission/execution contract.

Nests A, B, C1, D, C2, E, F, I, J, K and L become `Noop`; P, dP and dS are never written. Leave
the original score matrix to its current placement/recompute policy. The acceptance claim is
removal of quadratic **gradient** intermediates, not necessarily removal of every quadratic
allocation in the training program. Test low and high recompute caps, including a materialized
score input, and attribute score recomputation separately in the report.

### As implemented (PR 1)

`Online_softmax.find_backward`, run in the same pass as the normalizer it anchors on (the
fixpoint's later rounds see no normalizer, so a backward can never match there). Where it departs
from the text above, with the evidence:

- **Three nests, not two.** dQ (query rows outer: `p`, `dp`, `ds`, then the `d` loop), dK (keys
  outer: the same scalars, then the `d` loop) and dV (keys outer: `p` alone, then the `e` loop).
  Stage 1's lane geometry (#883) reads a nest as `Grid` prefix → serial loop → ONE `Workgroup`
  loop after a loop-free scalar preamble; a preamble followed by two sibling channel loops gets
  no lanes, so dK and dV no longer share one. dV needs no `dp`, so its preamble is loop-free
  wherever `p`'s instantiation is (the scores stored) — lane-eligible. dQ and dK carry `dp`'s
  value-width reduction in their preamble and so take no lanes: stage 1 measured a loop-bearing
  preamble (the value pass recomputing `q·k` per lane) as a 1.5x Metal regression at seq 1024,
  and admitting `dp` into the preamble would be the same trade unmeasured. A cooperative `dp`
  (a `Workgroup_reduce` over the value width shared by the lanes) is the follow-up that would give
  dQ and dK lanes. The extra cost of the split is one `p` recompute per pair.
- **Where `p` is read from.** The dQ nest reconstructs `p = e / l`, the dK nest `p = exp(n) / l`,
  and the dV nest instantiates the chain past `x` down to the scores' reduction — the forward's own
  definitions at each nest's subscripts, each stopping at a different node. The visit cap counts a
  read in a `Set_local` but exempts one in a `Set` at its own write position; `x` is already read by
  the scan, so more scope-local reads of it made the masked scores a stored `[seq, seq]` buffer
  (probe at head width 8). One counted read per node keeps every node of the chain virtual.
- **Precision.** One precision per side: `P` at the scores', the gradient chain `dP .. dS` at one
  of its own (the default gradient precision may differ from a double model's), the locals at
  the wider state precision.
- **Where the nests go.** At the first consumed nest (the `dP` reduction); every node they read
  must be final there, and nothing else may touch `q.grad`, `k.grad` or `v.grad` between that
  point and the composed contraction that wrote each. A scalar the chain reads but that is
  (re)filled later in the span -- the masked branch's zero -- enters as its constant.
- **At head width 32** (leg-6 model, `online_softmax` on): the training step's `[seq, seq]` writes
  go from the scores, `P`, `dP` and `dS` to the scores alone, which are routine scratch; at head
  width 8, or with the recompute cap raised, to none.
- **Found on the way**: `Online_softmax.lowest_finite` at f64 was Base's `Float.max_value`, which
  is infinity, so the forward's floor was `-inf` and a masked prefix at f64 went NaN; fixed, with
  an executed f64 leg.

## 1003: a single-pass, block-tiled forward fold

### Stages 0 and 1: re-measure, then fix the hoisted nests' schedule

Stage 0 re-runs the gh-483 matrix on Metal post-#995 (and CUDA/HIP through the sweep),
attributing time per fission segment, and checks whether the stored-score matmul is seeded and
timed as tensorized in the tuned arm (`autotune_log`, `report.mma_timed`). Stage 1 lets the inner
parallel loop of a hoisted nest map to `Workgroup` lanes under the serial key loop:
`Grid (b,s,h) → Serial t → Workgroup e`, `p` lane-uniform and each lane owning `O[s,e]`. Either
the default GPU annotator learns to take a parallel loop nested under the serial reduction loop
when the prefix is short of `block_size`, or it is a sketch seed; `validate_parallel` must accept
a `Workgroup` loop under a `Serial` reduction loop. It recovers the composed form's thread count
and benefits 1002's dV nest identically (its dQ and dK nests carry `dp`'s reduction in their
preamble, which the rule excludes; see "As implemented (PR 1)").

### The block computation

For query block `I` and key block `J`, carry row maxima `m`, row sums `l`, and an unnormalized
output numerator `U`. Initially `m = -inf`, `l = 0`, `U = 0`:

```text
S_tile = score_expression(Q_I * transpose(K_J))
m_new  = max(m, rowmax(S_tile))
a      = exp(m - m_new)
P_tile = exp(S_tile - m_new)
l_new  = a * l + rowsum(P_tile)
U_new  = a[:, None] * U + P_tile * V_J
# after the last key block:
O_I    = U / l[:, None]
```

This is the block recurrence in
[FlashAttention-2, Algorithm 1](https://tridao.me/publications/flash2/flash2.pdf).
The displayed arithmetic is for ordinary finite, nonempty rows; emission must retain the
repository's special-value behavior below.

Both query and key axes need blocking: a single query row does not fill an MMA output tile.
Map independent batch/head/query blocks to the grid, and execute key blocks serially within
each owner. The score tile and probability tile stay in registers/shared memory and are reused
for the value contraction before the next key block. This eliminates the second score pass of
the current forward. Blocking only its normalizer is a useful intermediate checkpoint, not the
completed issue.

### Tile state: `U` as a `Local` tile node

The member emits, when `online_softmax_block` is nonzero, per query block a `Scan_loop` over key
blocks whose scalar carries are `(m, l)` per row, and whose body writes `S_tile` and `P_tile` as
minted `Local` tile nodes, rescales `U` by α, and accumulates `P_tile · V_J` into `U` with
`Tile_mma` (score tile likewise). `U` is an undeclared `Local` tile node updated in place. This is
sound without new IR: scan-body writes are refused as virtualization candidates
(`Non_virtual 148`), so `U` is never inlined across iterations, and schedule ops do not enter a
scan body, so nothing reorders the rescale against the accumulation.

Rejected: scalar-expanded `U` as scan carries (`Bq·dv` carries per scan — thousands — and MMA
fragments cannot be named as scalars). Postponed to #1122: the associative-combine licence on
`Scan_loop` and declared carried-through-memory nodes. Replacing per-key updates with block
reductions needs an associativity claim, which is why the fold is emitted by the numerics-gated
rewrite rather than derived by a schedule transform; a licence would quantify over the full
carried state, so `U` must be declared before licensing, not before tiling.

### Scheduling and code generation

The block size is a rewrite-time key `online_softmax_block` (0 = today's two-pass form), read
under the existing `online_softmax` gate. It is `Code_borne`: the autotuner rebases one captured
lowering per candidate and cannot vary it — an accepted limitation. Choosing the geometry at the
post-optimization scheduling seam (a `Block_scan` op and an attention sketch family) is #1123,
to be done only if the measurements below justify it.

1. Prove scalar block-fold parity, including grouped query rows, before introducing cooperation.
   Include the single-key-block and block-size-one controls.
2. Stage `Q`, `K`, and `V` in layouts accepted by both contractions. Reuse one cooperating lane
   domain for score MMA, row max/sum, exponentiation, and value MMA. Handle transitions between
   cooperative fragments and row-wise scalar work explicitly. A conservative shared-tile
   store/reload path — `U` loaded, rescaled and stored around each `Tile_mma` — is a valid first
   implementation if its barriers and owners are proved.
3. Support accumulator rescaling and tile lifetimes in `C_syntax` and the backend MMA hooks.
   The current persistent-fragment matcher does not understand this recurrence; the
   fragment-resident rescale is a later, separately measured step. Provide a correct scalar
   fallback and preserve accumulator precision at every transfer boundary.
4. Gate the tiled form on hardware capability and resource limits: on a backend or operand
   triple without MMA support, `Tile_mma` takes its equivalent fallback. `P_tile` is newly
   computed floating-point data: narrowing it for an MMA input is a numerical choice, not a free
   layout conversion. Do not silently enable TF32 or another conversion to make an f32 cell
   tensorize.
5. Preserve eligibility and tile geometry in canonical rendering and cache identity (the key is
   `Code_borne`, so the emitted code carries it). Include cold compilation, warm replay and
   sibling-candidate isolation tests.

Keep the working scratch bound proportional to
`Bq*Bk + Bq*dq + Bk*(dq+dv) + Bq*dv`, independent of the full key count for fixed tile sizes.
Budget every simultaneously live buffer, including staged copies and any double buffering;
defer async pipelines until the synchronous path is measured.

### Masks, tails, and compatibility

Masked and padded key positions must have zero exponential weight; zero-padding their *scores*
would add attention mass. Apply head-dimension zero fringe before the dot product and key-mask
semantics afterwards. Keep barriers and MMA under uniform control for partial query/key blocks;
per-element predicates suppress invalid loads/stores. Skip a wholly causal-masked block only
when its identity effect is proved for all live rows and the accepted value semantics.

Retain the current composed behavior for masked prefixes, all-`-inf` rows (NaN), finite fills,
lowest-finite scores, and NaNs. Extend the finite-floor rescaling carefully to the block maximum
and to `U`, and test positive infinities against the composed reference too. Avoid manufacturing
NaNs via identities such as `l + (m - m)` that the simplifier can reassociate. Fast-math is a
separate executed test regime. A mask optimization must not silently suppress a NaN that the
reference computation would observe.

Emit final `(m,l,O)` with the shared saved-state meanings, so either composed or fused backward
can follow. Bypass a full score buffer only when the matched region makes that legal; explicit
materialization or extra readers retain their semantics. Active dropout and unsupported
score/probability transformations take the existing forward path initially.

### As implemented (1003 PR 1: the scalar fold)

`Online_softmax.find_fold`, the pass's fourth shape, under a positive `online_softmax_block` (`B`); a
normalizer it declines keeps the two-pass form. Per query block of `B` rows, per row, one
`Scan_loop` over the key blocks of `B` carrying the row's `(m, l)`, whose body computes the row's
block of scores into a tile `S[B]` (the score reduction transplanted from the composed nest, then
the recovered scale/mask chain), the new max, `α`, `P = exp(S - m')` in place, `l'`, the rescale
`U *= α` and `U += P · V_blk` into a tile `U[d_v]`; the last key block writes `O = U / l`.
Parity against the composed and the two-pass forms at `B` 1, 3, 5, 8, 16, 32 over sequence lengths
that divide, leave tails and fit one block, rectangular queries/keys, the special-value fills, two
stacked blocks, and training under both backwards (treatments E and F): all in
`test/operations/online_softmax_block.ml`, rerun under `cc_backend_fast_math`. Where it departs
from the text above, with the evidence:

- **The tiles are per row, `S[B]` and `U[d_v]`, not `[B, B]` and `[B, d_v]`.** The query rows are
  the loop around the scan (one carried pair per row, no unrolling), so a row touches only its own
  slice. Per-row tiles written whole ahead of the scan, inside the row loop, satisfy both
  renderers' rules at once: cc's pool-parallel `Grid` privatizes a local per chunk only when its
  first access is a standalone covering write, and the GPU annotator's chain follows single-child
  loops. A block-row tile needs either a block-wide init beside the row loop, which ends the chain
  above the row loop (`B` times fewer threads), or a per-row init, which covers only its row (no
  privatization, a serial cc kernel). The block-row, workgroup-shared layout is what the
  cooperative rendering needs, and it is that transform's to impose.
- **Where it goes.** At the value pass: the lowering computes `V` after the softmax chain, so the
  fold cannot sit at the max as the two-pass scan does. The elementwise definitions between the max
  and the value pass that read the row state -- the shifted scores, the exponentials, the
  probabilities -- move behind it when a later statement reads them (the training step's composed
  backward), and go when nothing does, together with the score chain the fold no longer reads (at
  head width 32 the recompute cap would otherwise store a dead `[seq, seq]` score buffer). `(m, l)`
  are written to their nodes only when something reads them after the fold.
- **No `Tile_mma` yet.** `Tile_mma` is constructed by schedule transforms only, after the
  optimization pipeline, and schedule transforms do not enter a scan body -- the very premise that
  makes the in-place `U` sound. So neither contraction in the body can be tensorized by `Tensorize`
  as it stands: the cooperative rendering is a schedule-side transform of its own, which recognizes
  the fold and rewrites it on capable GPUs (next paragraph). This PR is the record's step 1.
- **Tails.** A key tail pads the score tile with `-inf` (zero weight) and guards the reads a padded
  cell would make (`k` in the score, `v` in the value update); a query tail guards the row's scan.
- **What the cooperative rendering needs** (the record's steps 2-4, not in this PR). The row loop
  around the scan becomes the lane loop: a `Workgroup` loop of the SIMD width, the rows beyond `B`
  idle in the row-wise work; each lane runs its row's scan, in lockstep with the others, so the
  carried `(m, l)` stay per-lane scalars. The tiles take the block's rows and move to
  workgroup-shared memory (`S[B, B]`, `U[B, d_v]`). In the body, the score slice becomes a per-row
  zeroing, a barrier and one `Tile_mma` over the block (`lane` = the row loop, `ta`/`tb` from the
  operands' layouts: `K` is read transposed), a barrier, then the per-row chain, max, exponentials
  and rescale; the value update becomes a barrier and one `Tile_mma` `U += P · V_blk` (the
  store/reload path: `U` is loaded, updated and stored per key block). `validate_parallel` already
  accepts a `Tile_mma` inside a scan inside its lane loop (its coverage and uniformity rules look
  through the scan), as long as no guard encloses a barrier -- so a query or key tail keeps the
  scalar form in v1. It is GPU-only: cc renders a `Workgroup` loop serially, which would run lane
  0's whole scan before lane 1's while the `Tile_mma` reads every lane's row. `Tensorize` itself
  cannot build this (`rewrite_loop` does not enter a scan, and its minted lane loop would nest a
  second `Workgroup` slot inside the row loop), so it is a new op the default GPU preset emits
  for a fission segment holding the fold, gated on the MMA capability for both operand triples.
- **Soundness, pinned.** The tiles request no memory mode, and the optimizer refuses them by
  structural rules, never only by a policy cap: every setter in the scan body is the scan's
  `Non_virtual 148`, and the whole-tile init ahead of the scan (per row) is a write the enclosing
  row loops repeat, `147`, which is recorded first once the caps are lifted (under the default
  caps the recompute or the visit cap records first, and a refused node is not examined again).
  An `Unroll` naming a loop inside the fold's scan body declines with the no-such-loop refusal.
  Both in leg 6 of the test.
